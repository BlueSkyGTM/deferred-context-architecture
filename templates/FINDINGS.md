# Findings

What was noticed, by whom, and what it would take to act on it. Nothing here has been acted on.
That is the point of the file.

`../foundations/authority.md`: a woken agent proposes and does not settle. Structural change is
filed here and waits for a ruling, because level five cannot promote itself and an agent that
cannot see the ladder cannot know it is at the bottom of one.

A finding is not a complaint and not a plan. It is a **compressed observation offered upward**,
written so the party with the picture can rule on it in one reading.

## Rules

- **A finding describes. It does not perform.** If the change was made, this was not a finding.
- **One finding, one observation.** Two things noticed at once are two entries. A finding that
  needs the word "and" in its title is two rulings wearing one coat.
- **Stated as a fact about the tree**, checkable by someone who was not there. "This folder is
  named for its output" is a finding. "This structure is confusing" is an opinion.
- **Name what acting on it would take**, concretely enough that the operator can decline it. A
  finding with no proposed change cannot be ruled on, only worried about.
- **Nothing here is ordered by importance.** Ranking findings is a judgment about the whole, and
  whoever filed them could not see the whole.
- **A declined finding stays filed.** Deleting it loses the fact that it was noticed and
  considered, which is the thing that stops it being raised every session.
